On collaborative work & co-authorship

Although the work on this website is filed under my name, the projects and related research are inherently collaborative and co-authored. To present these endeavors as a sole author would be extractive. Co-authorship acknowledges the shared labour, resources, and intellectual contributions involved across all initiatives.
